# Retry gate evaluation on 600 fresh questions

Both newly trained gates beat retry-on-invalid on observed accuracy and solver-token compute. The margin-trained gate with the arithmetic check delivered the most correct answers. The tighter gate with the arithmetic check used the fewest solver tokens among the retry policies.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Compact only | 388/600 | 64.67% | 318.43 | 0 | 9.44% |
| Retry on invalid | 397/600 | 66.17% | 351.61 | 53 | — |
| Previous frozen gate | 406/600 | 67.67% | 348.89 | 67 | 0.77% |
| Retry on invalid with arithmetic check | 404/600 | 67.33% | 351.61 | 53 | 0.00% |

| Tighter gate with arithmetic check | 404/600 | 67.33% | 336.85 | 49 | 4.20% |

Mean compute includes all model input and output tokens for the executed path, scaled to Qwen2.5-3B-equivalent tokens. The first compact pass accounts for 90.56% of the baseline’s solver tokens. The tighter gate saved 37.44% of retry tokens on its own and 44.47% with the arithmetic check.

The margin-trained gate alone gained 1.50 percentage points over retry-on-invalid, with a paired 95% bootstrap interval of +0.50 to +2.67 points. With the arithmetic check, its gain was 1.67 points, with an interval of +0.67 to +2.83. Against retry-on-invalid equipped with the same check, it gained three correct answers and saved 0.97% compute. Against the previous frozen gate, it gained one answer and saved 0.20% compute.

Both versions of the tighter gate gained 1.17 percentage points over retry-on-invalid, with a paired 95% interval of −0.17 to +2.50 points. The tighter gate with the check gained 12 math answers and lost five science answers relative to retry-on-invalid. It matched the accuracy of retry-on-invalid equipped with the same check and used 4.20% less compute. It used 3.45% less compute than the previous frozen gate and scored two fewer answers.

The arithmetic check submits a recomputed value when the compact solver’s last complete equation reports its final answer and the preceding equality terms agree on a different exact value. Seven of its eight submissions fixed a wrong compact answer. All policies preserved every initially correct answer. All eight arithmetic submissions replaced scheduled retries in the tighter gate, preserving its per-question correctness while saving 1,081 raw model tokens.

## Training and test protocol

The test set has 300 GSM8K math questions and 100 each in MMLU-Pro biology, chemistry, and physics. It excludes IDs and normalized question text used in the earlier experiments. All policies used the same Qwen3.5-4B weights, compact and retry prompts, generation limits, and response parsers. The arithmetic variants changed submission decisions using the text already generated by the compact solver. One local Ollama run collected all 600 first responses and the union of 90 requested retries.

The margin-trained fit used 1,520 archived pairs and 249 stratified training probes. The probes reproduced every archived response and token count. Rich token probabilities were available during training. The deployed calibration uses the compact call’s original selected-token confidence fields. On the 180 fresh development pairs, average precision for finding a retry that could fix an answer was 0.304 for the selected gate and 0.135 for the invalid-answer flag. Model selection also used the previous 300-question benchmark after filling in its 267 missing paired responses.

The tighter gate fit on all 1,820 complete pairs. It predicts accepted fixes and regressions directly and caps retry work at 65% of the batch’s retry-on-invalid input-token budget. It gained four correct answers on the 180 fresh development pairs and passed separate out-of-fold accuracy checks on the 1,520 archived pairs and the earlier 300 completed pairs.

All models and arithmetic variants were frozen before test outcomes were scored. The plans were determined from the questions and first responses before collecting any test retries. The packed archive reproduces the three freezes, plans, and scores byte for byte. An independent check reconciled grading and token charges for all 4,800 policy outcomes.
